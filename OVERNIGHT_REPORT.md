## 5. Remaining Items, Recorded Assumptions & Next Steps

### Recorded Engineering Assumptions
1. **In-Memory Lockout & JTI Revocation Store**: Scoped for the locked single-node `node:sqlite` architecture (documented in `docs/DEPLOYMENT_RUNBOOK.md`); restarts clear expired lockout windows and require rotating `JWT_SECRET` for global emergency session invalidation.
2. **Institutional Email Preservation in PII Scrubber**: `dataPipelineValidator.ts` intentionally preserves public institutional `.edu.kh` emails (e.g., `admissions@cadt.edu.kh`) while masking personal emails, Cambodian phone numbers (`0xx`/`+855`), and Telegram handles (`t.me/*`, `@handle`).
3. **Guest-to-Account Bookmark Merge**: Upon student login/registration, guest `localStorage` bookmarks (`csip_saved_scholarships`) are merged into SQLite via `POST /api/scholarships/saved/sync` and cleared locally so multi-student browser sessions maintain strict isolation.

1. **Minor / Guardian Verification Workflow**: Define the operational process for approving guardian verification requests via `POST /api/privacy/parent-verify/:studentId/approve`; until approved, minor optional data collection stays fail-closed.
2. **Production Secrets & TLS Proxy**: Provision persistent `JWT_SECRET` (`>= 32` chars), `RESEARCH_SALT`, `FRONTEND_ORIGIN`, optional `GEMINI_API_KEY`, and HTTPS termination (keeping default `COOKIE_SECURE=true`) per `docs/DEPLOYMENT_RUNBOOK.md`.
3. **PR Review & Merge**: Review the open Draft Pull Request (`feat/phases-4-6-remediation` -> `main`), verify GitHub Actions CI passes on the PR, and merge when ready for staging deployment.
